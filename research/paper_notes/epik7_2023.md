# Epik 7 (Schrödinger)

## Citation
Johnston R. C. et al. "Epik: pKa and Protonation State Prediction through Machine Learning."
ChemRxiv 64188769dab08ad68f6bc895 (2023); published J. Chem. Theory Comput. (version not confirmed
in this session). Vendor page: https://www.schrodinger.com/science-articles/schrodinger-solutions-
small-molecule-protonation-state-enumeration-and-pka. Checked 2026-10-26.

## Fulltext status
abstract_only + vendor technical page (full paper NOT read). Method details below come from the
abstract and Schrödinger's own description; do not treat as peer-reviewed methodology detail.

## Claims (as stated by the source)
- "an ensemble of atomic graph convolutional neural networks (GCNNs) trained on over 42,000 pKa
  values across broad chemical space from both experimental and computed origins" (abstract).
- "Epik version 7 also generates protonation states and recovers 95% of the most populated
  protonation states compared to previous versions" (abstract).
- "Requiring on average only 47 ms per ligand" (abstract).
- Vendor: micro-pKa from "3-layer atomic graph convolutional neural networks (GCNNs) extending out
  radially six bonds from the ionizing atom"; "The predicted pKa values for the states are then
  used to predict the relative energies of the states"; Macro-pKa "rank[s] the protonation states
  by energy" and can "return the most populated states for a user-supplied pH".
- **3D**: "The topological nature of the ML approach means that Epik 7, like previous versions, is
  rapid but agnostic to 3D geometry and stereochemistry."

## Relevance
Industry-standard solution to task B (plausible microstates + state penalties at a pH) that is
explicitly 2D/topological. It cannot condition on conformation, and it outputs state energies, not
validated equilibrium populations.
