## Method (abstract-level)
Diffusion process on the hypertorus of torsion angles; conformers are generated by sampling torsions given a
local structure, rather than diffusing Cartesian coordinates.
## Relevance to p(z,x|G,h)
This is the x-side baseline to be extended. The conditioning graph G (including element identities, formal charges
and the hydrogen/protonation pattern) is an INPUT and is fixed throughout sampling; nothing in the method selects
a protonation microstate. Extending it to p(z, tau | G, h) means adding a discrete z variable that the torsion
denoiser conditions on and that is itself sampled.
