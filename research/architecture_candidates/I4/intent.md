We hypothesize that a benchmark scoring (microstate, conformer) pairs at a specified pH — state validity, state recall/precision against curated microstate sets, per-state geometry quality against state-specific reference ensembles, and experimental macro-pKa/tautomer-ratio recovery stratified by conformational sensitivity — exposes failure modes of current conformer generators and pKa predictors that existing single-protomer conformer benchmarks (GEOM) and geometry-free pKa benchmarks (SAMPL) cannot detect.
