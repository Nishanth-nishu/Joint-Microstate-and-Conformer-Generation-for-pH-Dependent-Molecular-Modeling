We hypothesize that a small learned correction ΔG(z | conformer ensemble), trained on experimental pKa and tautomer ratios and applied on top of an external microstate enumerator plus a 2D free-energy predictor, improves pH-dependent microstate population prediction on scaffold-held-out conformationally sensitive molecules over the same 2D predictor without the geometry input, because geometry-dependent effects (intramolecular hydrogen bonds, charge–charge contacts) are systematic residuals of topology-only models.
