## Answer (JOINT)
EXP-1 revised (§10.4).
1. **Primary A6b = A0 fine-tuned for E_D = 20 epochs** on the re-matched sampler-L data, with the same optimiser and
   schedule as A5's DAgger stage. It is therefore compute-matched to A5 (100 + E_D). **A6b-full**, an S5-style
   100-epoch retrain, is secondary and is run only if A5 − A6b is significant, to rule out under-training.
   E_D = 20 is my design choice; no source gives it.
2. **Re-matching cost: still UNVERIFIED.** The orchestrator should supply the round-1 `standardize_confs.py` CPU-h
   and worker count. It sets the M2 ask (EXP-1 §9 item 4).
3. **Fresh ETKDG embeddings** made as `standardize_confs.py` makes them, then conformer matching. Not the cache's
   `data.pos[k]`: its torsions are already matched to the target GT, so a τ-aware geometry head would see GT-matched
   torsions in training but raw ETKDG torsions at test. That is a leak plus a mismatch. This is the S5 recipe with a
   new L source [E-JOINT-006]. The same applies to A5/A8 DAgger roll-outs.
