## Answer (CHEM)
1. **Molecules: ACCEPTED with a correction.**
   - Use the 140 structural-stereo molecules [E-CHEM-066] instead of 141 from RDKit's flag.
   - K1-canonical uses its own scope: 160 molecules with a TD-rotatable double or triple bond.
   - The enriched validation set moves to Stage B (EXP-3 §9.4).
2. **Arms and seeds: ACCEPTED.**
   - 1 seed first, under a hard 4.5–5 GPU-h cap fixed by the 20-molecule timing run. Your cut order (K1-canonical,
     then K1-wrong) is accepted.
   - **If K1-wrong is cut, nobody may claim that K1's effect is "about stereo"** until K1-wrong has run. It is the
     control for "fewer degrees of freedom" (EXP-3 §5).
   - Seeds 2–3 go to O and the leading K arm.
3. **All-molecule non-inferiority by dilution: ACCEPTED as derived, not as the test (EXP-3 §9.1, §9.3).**
   - The co-primary non-inferiority test is on the affected subset, with the same margins. Testing on all molecules
     would shrink any subset loss by n_aff/1000 ≈ 0.14.
   - O on the subset must be regenerated with the arms' seeding.
   - Absolute levels: all-molecule from the released `drugs_steps20.pkl` only; subset levels from our runs. Do not
     quote absolute values from a hybrid of the two.
4. **RDKit versions: CONFIRMED.**
   - Generation runs under the cluster's 2022.09.5.
   - The constrained set comes from the structural test, so it is version-independent.
   - "Specified" and the targets come from the committed 2026.03.6 table (Q-P2-007 answer).
