# Q-P2-006 (from coding planner P2, round 5) → R-CHEM (cc R-JOINT)

**Concerns:** EXP-1 §2.8 "E/Z" ("Exp 1 measures it with `tools/stereo_check.py` and adopts whatever Exp 3 decides …
Consult `round5/consult/Q-JOINT-001.md` (to be filed by the coding agents)"), EXP-1 §5.4 Q-*-002; EXP-3 §3.2 arms.

**Question.** Which Exp 3 mechanism should the FlexiTors V2 sampler inherit for TD-rotatable double bonds in Stage B:
K1 freeze (inference only, no retraining), K2 (reflected kernel, needs retraining of the torsion head, which V2
retrains anyway), or none until Exp 3's QM9/DRUGS inference arms report? And should the geometry head's P_W rows for
those bonds stay (they then keep the double-bond dihedral fixed to first order during L steps)?

**Decision at stake:** whether V2 training (Stage B) must already include the K2 training transform, and whether the
Exp 1 primary arms are compared with or without an E/Z constraint.

**Default used (PENDING Q-P2-006):** Stage A only measures E/Z and double-bond planarity on Exp 1 Gate-0 outputs with
the shared `flexitors/eval/stereo_eval.py`; W rows for double bonds are kept; no constraint in Exp 1 until Exp 3's
inference arms are analysed.

## Answer (CHEM)
**Decision (EXP-3 §9.5).**
- **Inherited operator.** V2 inherits K1 or K2-inf by the pre-declared rule: highest DRUGS E/Z fidelity among arms
  passing subset non-inferiority; K1 if the CIs overlap.
- **No K2 transform in V2 training** unless Exp 3's K2-train is tested first. Both candidate operators are
  inference-only (EXP-3 §3.2), so Stage B training does not depend on Exp 3.
- **P_W rows for TD-rotatable double bonds: keep them.** They hold the double-bond dihedral fixed to first order,
  which is consistent with K1 (EXP-1 §2.2 after D-004).
- **How Exp 1 arms are compared.** The operator is applied identically to every arm, the A1 baseline included, so it
  cancels in the contrasts. Every primary arm is also reported without it.
- **Your Stage A default is accepted:** measure only, with the shared `flexitors/eval/stereo_eval.py` and no
  constraint.

**Reason.** TD alone gives 10–12 % E/Z-absent-from-GT conformers and 16.6° mean non-planarity [E-CHEM-061,
E-CHEM-063]. A shared, inference-only fix keeps Exp 1's geometry contrasts unconfounded.
