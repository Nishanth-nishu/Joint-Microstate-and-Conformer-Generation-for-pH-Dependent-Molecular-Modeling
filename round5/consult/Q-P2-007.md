## Answer (CHEM)
**Decision: (a) and (b) ACCEPTED, and 2026.03.6 defines "specified" (EXP-3 §3.1 revision, §9.3).**

New fact behind the decision [E-CHEM-066, `chem_data/structural_subsets.out`, `slash_check.out`]:
- The QM9 version gap is explicit-H imines. All 67 molecules that 2026.03.6 calls "specified" are written
  `[H]/N=C…`: RDKit counts the explicit H as a stereo neighbour, but the N has one heavy neighbour.
- By the structural test (TD-rotatable, order 2, both atoms ≥ 2 heavy neighbours), QM9 has **37** stereo molecules,
  agreeing with 2022.09.5's 38. Only **3** are specified.
- DRUGS: 140 structural, 126 specified.

Consequences:
- **Primary label:** the geometric label (sign of cos δ_b on TD's reference quadruple) against a committed target
  table, built once with 2026.03.6 and keyed by SMILES and `MolFromSmiles`+`AddHs` atom indices, with your stability
  test.
- **Secondary:** RDKit-3D labels under both versions.
- **Primary denominator:**
  - DRUGS: the 126 specified molecules;
  - QM9: consistency with the GT ensemble on the 37 structural molecules (a SMILES target exists for only 3).
- **Reproduction subset:** the 2022.09.5 one (38 molecules), as you propose.
- **Imine N–H bonds** are classed `double_nonstereo`: planarity only, and they do not count toward the E/Z endpoint,
  because heavy-atom RMSD cannot see them.
