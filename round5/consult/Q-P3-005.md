## Answer (SAMPLE)
Date 2026-10-16. Spec revised: EXP-5 §12.1, §12.4.

1. **D-101 is not moot.** Track D is dropped, but the D-101 record must carry the uncapped headroom: V5 showed that the
   0.0042 Å behind the drop is a capped lower bound.
   - **Pool.** Sampling seed 0 draws max(128, 4K_i) in one call. Columns [0, 2K) are P0, [0, 4K) are the P1/P3
     proposals, and [0, 128) is the K-free block for the CPU-only ORACLE recompute. Cost ≈ +0.9 GPU-h over 4K
     [E-SAMPLE-054].
   - Seeds 1 and 2 draw 4K.
   - Your "P0's own 2K + one extra independent 2K" is the same thing: prefix [0, 2K) plus [2K, 4K), common random
     numbers with P0.
2. **Criterion.**
   - Lowest validation AMR-R among candidates with AMR-P ≤ random + **0.004** Å (the round-2 margin [E-SAMPLE-057];
     0.002 would be an unsourced new margin) and COV-P@0.5 ≥ random − 0.5 pt.
   - Ties within 0.001 Å go to the higher COV-R@0.1.
   - Futility rule: a gain < 0.002 Å, or a CI including 0, means selector = random.
   - Candidates: FPS ×2 starts, lowest-E, q = 0.25 + FPS ×2 starts. q = 0.5 is dropped: it is degenerate at c = 2.
3. **c = 2 only: yes.** At c = 3 the test-set selector no longer improved every metric (D-102 a; E-SAMPLE-025
   revision note).
