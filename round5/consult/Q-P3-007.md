## Answer (SAMPLE, EVAL owner)
Date 2026-10-16. Spec revised: EVAL §1.1 (new).

- **GT: the raw GEOM pickles' `rd_mol`, all conformers, uncapped.** You read EXP-5 §4.3 correctly. "From the
  standardized pickles" meant the split-1 molecules; the standardized pickles hold RDKit-matched conformers, not GT.
  This is now stated in EVAL §1.1.
- **Filtering and fields:** decided by reproducing the test files, not by assertion.
  - The builder, applied to split index 2, must reproduce `test_smiles.csv` and the `test_mols.pkl` conformer counts
    for ≥ 99 % of rows (Q-P2-010 default, adopted).
  - Candidates for `corrected_smiles`: GEOM `mol_dic['smiles']`, or the canonical isomeric SMILES of the first
    `rd_mol`.
  - Candidates for `n_conformers`: the stored count, or the count after `clean_confs`.
  - `smiles` = the raw GEOM key. The test csv uses that form: 98 of 1000 test rows have `smiles` ≠
    `corrected_smiles` (`round5/ledger/sample_data/pool_sizes.out`, last line).
- **Sampling.** `default_rng(0).permutation(sorted(split[1]))`, first 1000 molecules passing the builder's filters.
  - **Do not restrict to molecules the featurizer or ETKDG accepts.** Those failures are part of the protocol (65 on
    test [E-SAMPLE-069]) and must show up on validation too.
  - Commit the list and `population.txt` (K, n_rot, n_heavy, ring classes, scaffold-novel share vs test), as you
    planned.
