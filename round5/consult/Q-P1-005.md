# Q-P1-005 (from coding planner P1, round 5) → R-CHEM (cc R-JOINT)

**Concerns:** EXP-1 §2.8 "E/Z" and §5.4 Q-*-002 (the consult EXP-1 asks the coding agents to file); EXP-3 §3.2.

## Question
EXP-1 says it "adopts whatever Exp 3 decides". Which Exp 3 operator should FlexiTors (V2 onwards) inherit by
default? The options are: K1 freeze of structural stereo double bonds, the K2-inf fold, or nothing until K2-train/K3
are tested. Is the choice taken from the Stage A QM9/DRUGS fidelity results with a pre-declared rule (for example,
the arm with the highest E/Z fidelity that passes AMR non-inferiority)?

## What depends on it
Nothing in Stage A. In M1, τ is frozen and every TD-rotatable double bond is a row of W, so the projector keeps its
dihedral fixed to first order. The answer fixes the V2/A4 sampler for M2.

## Default (PENDING)
Adopt Exp 3's best inference arm by the rule above; K1 if tied.

## Answer (CHEM)
**Decision.** Yes, choose by a pre-declared rule (EXP-3 §9.5).
- After Exp 3 Stage A, V2 inherits the inference arm (K1 or K2-inf) with the highest E/Z fidelity among those
  that pass subset non-inferiority: AMR-R +0.004 Å, COV-R@0.5 −0.5 points (EXP-3 §9.3).
- DRUGS decides; QM9 is used only if DRUGS results are missing.
- If the fidelity CIs overlap, choose K1: no extra NFE and no retraining.
- F-filter and F-flip are not inheritable, because their extra NFE would confound Exp 1's compute comparisons.
- Unspecified and non-stereo double bonds get only the late planarity fold, never the seed's isomer (EXP-3 §3.1
  revision, §9.2).

**Reasons.**
- Both candidate operators are inference-only (EXP-3 §3.2), so V2 training does not wait for Exp 3.
- Pinning RDKit's seed isomer for unspecified bonds is worse than TD's own choice: 147/526 vs 65/526 conformers with
  E/Z absent from GT [E-CHEM-061].
- M1 needs nothing: your reading is right. τ is frozen, and the P_W rows keep double-bond dihedrals fixed to first
  order (EXP-1 §2.2 after D-004).
