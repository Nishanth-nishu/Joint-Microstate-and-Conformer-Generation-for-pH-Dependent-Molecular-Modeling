## Answer (CHEM)
1. **Subset generation and splicing: ACCEPTED, with conditions (EXP-3 §9.1).**
   - Use per-molecule seeding.
   - **Regenerate O on the subset with the same seeding as the arms.** The released R_s conformers are only the
     common filler outside the subset.
   - Add a bit-identity test on unaffected molecules. For K1-canonical the affected set is the 211 double-or-triple
     molecules.
   - Quote absolute all-molecule levels from one source only.
   - Validity: on unaffected molecules the paired difference is exactly 0. The all-molecule difference is
     therefore (n_aff/N) × the subset difference, and the molecule bootstrap stays valid.
   - Subset sizes: QM9 102 molecules with a TD-rotatable double bond; 37 by the structural stereo test
     [E-CHEM-066].
2. **Primary family: CONFIRMED with two changes (EXP-3 §9.3).**
   - **Endpoint per dataset.** QM9's E/Z endpoint is consistency with the GT ensemble, on 37 molecules. Only 3 QM9
     molecules have specified heavy-atom stereo [E-CHEM-066]. Fidelity to the SMILES target is the DRUGS endpoint
     (126 molecules).
   - **Non-inferiority on the affected subset.** It is tested on the subset with the stated margins. The
     all-molecule figure is derived; testing on all molecules would dilute any loss by n_aff/N.
   - Holm over the 8 primary tests per dataset. Your secondary contrasts are confirmed.
3. **Step 0 ORACLE target: the nearest GT conformer.** That is, the column argmin of `eval.pkl` rmsd[:, k] for each
   generated conformer k. Report the majority GT label as a sensitivity row. Specified bonds use the SMILES target
   table, not the oracle.
4. **F-filter: fill to 2K, counted.**
   - Fill with the least-violating invalid conformers.
   - Count them as fidelity failures and report how many molecules needed a fill.
   - Reason: output without replacement worsens recall (AMR-R 0.2417 → 0.2472 on the stereo subset)
     [E-CHEM-064].
5. **K2-inf seeds.**
   - Uniform-then-fold gives a uniform angle on I, the K2 prior: **confirmed**.
   - Centre for unspecified and `double_nonstereo` bonds: **NOT the seed's state.**
     - Instead, use the late planarity fold: free until the first step with σ ≤ 0.1π, then fold around the nearest
       of {0, π} at that step (EXP-3 §3.1 revision, §9.2).
     - Reason: RDKit's random seed isomer is wrong far more often than TD's own choice (A0 147/526 vs R0 65/526)
       [E-CHEM-061].
     - The 0.1π switch is a pre-declared design choice, not a sourced number.
